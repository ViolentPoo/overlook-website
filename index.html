Inside OVERLOOK

Designed for the screen across the room.

Authentic application captures show the television-first interface in action. Guide listings and sports results are permanently blurred for public display.

A simpler way to watch

Less searching.
More discovering.

OVERLOOK brings the places you already watch into one thoughtful, television-first interface. Content and subscriptions are provided by their respective services.

Unified Search

Search configured Plex and Jellyfin libraries alongside supported Plex Discover information. Results depend on the media services you connect.

Live TV & Guide

Browse supported live television and program information with HDHomeRun integration.

Sports

Explore schedules and supported viewing destinations. Broadcast access and subscriptions are not included.

Streaming Applications

Open supported streaming applications installed on your device from a convenient central launcher. Installed apps still require their own access or subscriptions.

Personal Media Libraries

Optionally connect supported Plex and Jellyfin servers to bring your own movie and television libraries into the experience.

TV-First Design

Large-screen readability and focused Android TV remote navigation put comfort first.

Personalization

Keep supported favorites close and shape an entertainment home that feels like yours.

How it works

From setup to sofa.

Three straightforward steps, once OVERLOOK becomes publicly available.

Install

Install OVERLOOK on a compatible Android TV or Google TV device when it is publicly released.

Connect

Configure the supported entertainment sources, media servers, and installed applications you choose.

Enjoy

Browse live television, discover content, access applications, and navigate from one interface.

Why OVERLOOK

Television should feel effortless.

Entertainment has become scattered across inputs, applications, guides, and libraries. OVERLOOK is being designed to reduce that friction without deciding where you should watch.

It puts user choice first: your services, your media, and your favorites, arranged for comfortable remote-control navigation on the largest screen in your home.
