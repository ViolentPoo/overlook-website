About OVERLOOK

A calmer home for entertainment.

OVERLOOK is an Android TV launcher and entertainment discovery application designed around the way people actually use a television.

The idea

Bring the experience together.

Television viewing often means moving between live channels, streaming applications, sports schedules, and personal media libraries. OVERLOOK is being built to make those choices easier to reach from one streamlined interface.

It does not provide streaming subscriptions or host third-party content. Instead, it helps you navigate supported sources, applications, and media services you already choose to use.

Design principles

Made for ten feet away.

Television first

Readable at a distance, comfortable with a remote, and focused on the living room rather than a phone-sized interface.

Simple by design

Reduce unnecessary steps and visual noise so entertainment discovery feels clear and direct.

User choice

Support the media sources and installed applications users choose without implying that access or subscriptions are included.

Current status

OVERLOOK is in development.

Android TV and Google TV are the intended platforms. No public release date, pricing, or universal device compatibility has been announced.
